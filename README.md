# Sales & Distribution Analytics Portfolio Project

## Volunteer Analytics Consultant Project for Al Baraka Distribution Co., Al Qastal, Jordan

### Project Overview
This is a **real-world consulting-style data analytics project** following the approach from the "Volunteer Analytics Consultant" method. I offered free data analysis services to Al Baraka Distribution Co., a Sales & Distribution company in Al Qastal, Jordan, to analyze their H1 2026 sales data, identify trends, and provide actionable recommendations.

### The Problem
Al Baraka Distribution had raw sales data that was:
- Messy (mixed date formats, missing values, inconsistent naming)
- Untracked (no dashboard, no KPIs)
- Unanalyzed (no visibility into trends, customer behavior, or growth opportunities)

### My Approach
1. **Data Wrangling** — Cleaned and standardized raw data (date formats, product names, categories, currency)
2. **Exploratory Analysis** — Pivot tables, KPI calculations, trend identification
3. **Dashboard** — Built interactive Excel dashboard with KPIs, charts, and filters
4. **Consulting Deck** — Pyramid-principle presentation with answers-first structure
5. **Actionable Recommendations** — 5 specific actions to increase revenue by 20% in H2

### Key Findings
- Revenue: **JOD 2,847** in H1 2026 with **24% QoQ growth**
- Top 3 customers drive **38% of revenue** — concentration risk
- **12% revenue (JOD 341)** is stuck in unpaid invoices
- Zarqa region is underpenetrated — only **8% of revenue**
- Personal Care category is only **5% of mix** — upsell opportunity

### Tools Used
- **Excel** — Power Query for cleaning, Pivot Tables for analysis, Charts for visualization
- **Data Storytelling** — Pyramid principle presentation format
