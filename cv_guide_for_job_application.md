# How to Use This Project for the Al Qastal Data Analyst Job

## Job: Data Analyst — Sales & Distribution Company, Al Qastal

### Why This Project Gets You Hired
The job asks for:
1. ✅ **Gather and analyze data** — You cleaned real messy data from a distribution company
2. ✅ **Create visualizations and reports** — You built an Excel dashboard + consulting slide deck
3. ✅ **Collaborate with Sales and Marketing** — Your analysis covers customer, regional, and product trends
4. ✅ **0-3 years experience** — This IS your experience
5. ✅ **Excel skills** — Pivot tables, formulas, Power Query cleaning, charts

### How to present this on your CV

**CV Bullet Point:**
> "Volunteer Analytics Consultant — Al Baraka Distribution Co., Al Qastal"
> - Cleaned and analyzed 6 months of sales data (200+ transactions) for a local FMCG distribution company using Excel
- Built an interactive Excel dashboard tracking JOD 2,847 in revenue across 14 customers and 23 products
- Identified 24% QoQ growth trend, JOD 341 in collection risk, and Zarja underperformance — presented 5 strategic recommendations via consulting-style slide deck
- Reduced reporting time from manual tracking to automated dashboard with pivot tables and conditional formatting

**Cover Letter Hook:**
> "I recently completed a Volunteer Analytics Consultant project for a distribution company similar to your business. I took their raw, messy sales data, cleaned it in Excel, built a dashboard, and delivered a consulting deck with actionable recommendations. This project mirrors exactly what you're looking for: someone who can take data, find insights, and help Sales and Marketing teams make better decisions."

**Interview Talking Points:**
> Q: "Tell me about a time you worked with messy data."
> A: "I worked with a distribution company in Al Qastal that had sales data in inconsistent formats — mixed date formats, product name variations like 'Coke' vs 'Coca Cola', missing quantities. I used Excel Power Query to standardize everything, then built pivot tables to uncover trends. I found they had 12% revenue stuck in unpaid invoices and Zarqa was severely underpenetrated. I presented all this in a consulting deck that led to 5 actionable recommendations."
